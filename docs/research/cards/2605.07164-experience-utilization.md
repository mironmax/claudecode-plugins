# Rethinking Experience Utilization in Self-Evolving Language Model Agents (ExpWeaver)

arXiv [2605.07164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07164) v1, 8 May 2026. Harbin Institute of Technology, Huawei.

**Claim.** Letting the agent decide during reasoning when to retrieve
experience (by emitting a `[Retrieve]` token) beats injecting it once at the
start or at every step. The paper reports this across four frameworks, seven
backbones and three environment types. Under GRPO on Qwen3-4B/ALFWorld, it
scores 5.3 points above init-only and 8.0 above always-on.

**Conditions.**
- Models: GPT-5.2, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi-K2.5, Qwen3.5-397B-A17B,
  Qwen3-32B/14B/4B.
- Tasks: ALFWorld (134 tasks), WebShop, and six QA sets (100 questions each).
- Memory: the unchanged pipelines of ReasoningBank (insights), AWM
  (workflows), SkillRL (skills) and G-Memory (trajectories plus insights).
  Retrieval is top-3 (ReasoningBank) or top-6 (SkillRL).
- Who writes, and when: each framework's own online or offline construction;
  only usage timing changes.
- Horizon: one benchmark pass; averages over three runs.

**Applicability: partially.** The plugin already uses a mix of timings:
preload at session start (init-only), recall once per human prompt (a trigger
driven by the human turn, not by each step), and search and reads the agent
initiates (the ExpWeaver pattern). For a frontier consumer, the evidence is
weaker than the headline. Table 1 reports 0.00 retrievals per sample for
GPT-5.2 on ALFWorld, WebShop and HotpotQA under ReasoningBank. Figure 2
nonetheless shows GPT-5.2 at 86.57 (ExpWeaver) against 81.34 (no experience)
on ALFWorld, a gain that cannot come from retrieved experience. The paper
does not address this; the added prompt instruction is one untested
explanation. On WebShop, GPT-5.2 scores 65.18 with ExpWeaver against 67.33
always-on. Stronger models invoking memory less is itself a reported result
(§5.1).

**What it would change here.** It gives a measurable question to put to
existing logs. For endorsed nodes, compare the split of `via` between pushed
routes (`preload`, `full_read`, `ambient`) and pulled routes (`search`,
`read`), and the endorsement rate per exposure on each route. Tool-level
recall (task 01) is a new pushed route. Under this paper's framing, it should
be judged by whether it lifts endorsements per injection, not by how many
injections it makes.
