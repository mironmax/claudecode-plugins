# WikiSkill: Compiling Agent Experience into Persistent Knowledge for Skill Evolution

arXiv [2608.27454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27454) v1, 27 Aug 2026. Google Research, Virginia Tech.

**Claim.** Keeping a persistent wiki between immutable execution traces and
validation-gated skills improves skill evolution. With Gemini-3.5-Flash,
giving the skill proposer wiki access raises the average from 48.7% to 63.7%.
Also giving the *inference* agent wiki access during training lowers it to
60.9%.

**Conditions.**
- Models: Qwen-3.5-4B/9B, Qwen-3.6-27B, Gemma-4-31B, Gemini-3.5-Flash.
- Tasks: LiveMathematicianBench, SealQA, SpreadsheetBench, OfficeQA, ALFWorld.
- Memory: three layers. A raw layer (immutable traces), a wiki (pattern
  pages, an evolution log, and `skill-impact.md` recording every proposal's
  diff, score and accept/reject), and skills (SKILL.md injected in full into
  the system prompt).
- Who writes, and when: a Wiki Maintainer patch-edits the wiki every
  iteration from up to 8 sampled traces (at most 5 failing, 3 passing). A
  proposer makes one atomic skill edit, which is kept only if the validation
  score beats the best so far. The wiki is never rolled back.
- Horizon: iterations 0–7; three independent runs; paired bootstrap.

**Applicability: partially.** The architecture has close counterparts here:
the raw layer corresponds to session notes and the storage root's git
history, and the impact log to the chore `declined` trail (`kg_progress`,
`core/chores.py:declined_ids`). The gate does not carry over, because it
needs a validation split with ground truth; this plugin's only label is
sparse endorsement. The horizon of eight iterations is too short to test
whether a continuously patch-edited wiki degrades the way 2605.12978 reports
for consolidated stores.

**What it would change here.** Nothing new to build. One claim is testable
from `chores.jsonl` and the progress trail: that recording refusals prevents
re-litigation. Count how often an id named in a `declined` line is proposed
again as a chore target within the trail window. The paper's rejected-proposal
log is motivated by the same failure.

**Verification.** Read in full §1–5.2 (method, main results, ablation,
skill/wiki dynamics) and the sampling paragraph of Appendix C. §5.3 onward
(case study, conclusion) and the other appendices were not read.
