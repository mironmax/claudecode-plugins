# Agentic Context Engineering: Evolving Contexts for Self-Improving Language Models (ACE)

arXiv [2510.04618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618) v3, 29 Mar 2026. Zhang et al., SambaNova Systems and UC Berkeley.

**Claim.** Keeping context as itemized bullets that grow through localized
"delta" edits, rather than whole-context LLM rewrites, avoids "context
collapse" and improves over strong baselines: "+10.6% on agents and +8.6% on
finance". The collapse example: a Dynamic Cheatsheet context of 18,282 tokens
(accuracy 66.7) was rewritten to 122 tokens at the next step (57.1), below
the 63.7 no-adaptation baseline.

**Conditions.**
- Model: DeepSeek-V3.1 (non-thinking) for Generator, Reflector and Curator
  alike. Appendix results cover GPT-OSS-120B, GPT-5.1 and
  Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct.
- Tasks: AppWorld; FiNER and Formula (XBRL finance); DDXPlus and BIRD-SQL in
  the appendix.
- Memory: bullets carrying an id and helpful/harmful counters. Deltas are
  merged by non-LLM logic, with embedding de-duplication.
- Who writes, and when: offline over training data (up to 5 epochs), or
  online over the shuffled test split with batch size 1. There are up to 5
  Reflector rounds per sample.
- Horizon: one pass over a test split online, or ≤5 epochs offline.
- Ablation (App. A.5, AppWorld test-normal): with incremental updates the
  average is 70.3; without them, 56.9.

**Applicability: partially.** The plugin already stores itemized units
(nodes), merges by id, and never rewrites the whole graph with an LLM. It is
therefore already on the side of ACE's central design choice. ACE's own
tables show a limit that matters here: without reliable feedback, adaptation
can hurt. Offline ACE without labels on FiNER gains only +0.4, and online
Dynamic Cheatsheet without labels loses 3.7 on average. In this plugin,
feedback is sparse endorsement. ACE was also among the frameworks that
2605.12978 found degrading from peak over longer streams.

**What it would change here.** Little. The plugin records a "helped" signal
(`_useful_ts`, `useful.jsonl`) but nothing for a node that misled. ACE
carries both counters, but no ablation isolates the harmful counter, so this
paper gives no evidence that adding one would help. That remains an open
question, not a finding.
